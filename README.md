# FeeSeal

**The menu price is the bill price. Verified.**

FeeSeal helps restaurants publish clear prices and fee disclosures — and helps diners check that the printed menu and the bill match what was published.

---

## For diners

When you sit down:

1. Open the restaurant’s FeeSeal menu (or scan from a shared link).
2. Check the **published prices and fees** the restaurant sealed.
3. Capture the **printed menu** and your **receipt**.
4. Confirm what was read from the photos.
5. See a clear comparison: published menu · printed menu · bill.

If something doesn’t line up, FeeSeal flags a **potential discrepancy** for you to review — not a legal conclusion.

> FeeSeal identifies observable differences between documents. It does not determine legal liability or provide legal advice.

### What you can see

- Item prices and fee disclosures the restaurant published  
- Whether the paper menu and the bill appear to match that record  
- A link to the **sealed proof** of the published menu  

---

## For restaurants

Publish once. Give guests a digital menu they can trust.

1. Set your menu, prices, and fee disclosures (amount **and** purpose).  
2. Seal a version — FeeSeal records a verifiable fingerprint of that menu.  
3. Share your public menu page with guests.  
4. When diners check a meal, you get a clear picture of whether printed menus and bills still match what you published.

### Why it matters

Prices live in three places that should agree: what you publish, what’s printed on the table, and what’s on the bill. Menus go stale. Fees appear without clear disclosure. FeeSeal gives you a **system of record** for what you disclosed and when — so both sides argue from the same source, not from memory.

Built with Florida’s fee-disclosure rules in mind (including operations charges on menus, websites, and bills). Confirm current requirements with your counsel before making compliance claims.

## How the check works

| Source | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | Your published menu (sealed) |
| **B** | Photo of the physical menu |
| **C** | Photo of the bill / receipt |

| If they differ | What it may mean |
| --- | --- |
| A vs B | Printed menu may be out of date |
| A vs C | Bill may not match what was published |
| B vs C | Bill may not match what was on the table |
| All match | Verified — the expected outcome |

---

## Language we use

We say **potential discrepancy**, **no matching disclosure found**, and **review recommended**.

We never say “illegal fee,” “fraud,” “violation,” or “you can sue.”
